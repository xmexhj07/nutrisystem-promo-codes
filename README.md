## The fine print on auto-delivery: the $125 catch and the money-back guarantee

Auto-delivery carries real commitments that the sales pages don't lead with, and they change the math from the section above.

If you take the auto-delivery discount and later want out, Nutrisystem's own FAQ says you can cancel before the shipping cutoff — 6:00 p.m. ET the day before your order ships — via online chat or by phone. But there's a fee: cancel **after the first 7 days** and before paying for your second shipment, and Nutrisystem charges your payment method **$125**, which it describes as the auto-delivery discount you received on the first shipment. Deleting your account info doesn't stop the shipments; only an explicit cancellation through chat or phone does.

There is a safety net, though. Nutrisystem offers a **money-back guarantee**: try the plan for 7 days, and if you're not satisfied you can get a full refund by calling 1-800-727-8046. The limits matter — it applies only to your first initial 4-week plan, once per customer, and it **excludes Partner Plans, Meal Delivery Plans, and Club Advantage purchases**.

So the practical read on auto-delivery is: the discount is real and usually larger than anything else in this article, but treat it as a commitment. If you're at all unsure you'll continue past the first month, either cancel within the 7-day guarantee window or skip auto-delivery, take a code plus the cash back, and keep your flexibility.
